the Pulse logo or icons. A fork is welcome; give it its own name and icon so nobody mistakes it for an official build.

Official builds are the ones published by the repository owner. Questions: open an issue or contact the repository
owner through GitHub.

No trademark search has been done for "Pulse"; this policy describes how the project uses its identity, not a
registered right.
