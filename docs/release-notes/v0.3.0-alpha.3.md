# Clepso 0.3.0 alpha 3

The literary guild now lives in the Mac app.

- New original pixel characters in the Models portrait, roster and level track: the Opus owl maestro, Fable fox storyteller, Sonnet verse keeper and Haiku paper crane.
- Opus 5/5.5 and Fable 5/5.1 have different silhouettes, costumes and books. Other versions keep the base family art; unknown families get a neutral robot.
- Authored gestures and blinks replace the whole-body bob. Only the selected portrait animates. Pause/Resume, system Reduce Motion and hidden/inactive-view suspension keep the screen quiet.
- About explains the names and edition details. Artwork is original Clepso interpretation, not an official provider mascot or a capability claim; levels still come from all-time output tokens.
- The website reflects the shipped artwork and preserves the original art for comparison. Its level-up flourish remains a website demo.

The stepped toggle thumb, compact Account upload layout, Goals +/- correction, desktop/IDE integration, Codex reading, Calendar export and optional email accounts remain included. Existing preferences and consent are retained.

Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. The app and DMG are signed and notarized. Upgrade using the DMG or Homebrew;
automatic in-app updates are not enabled in this alpha.

For the complete implemented/future/verification inventory, see [implementation status](../STATUS.md).
