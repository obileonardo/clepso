About the artwork.

The literary guild is original Clepso pixel art inspired by the creative-work names Opus, Fable, Sonnet and Haiku. The owl maestro, fox storyteller, poet and paper crane are our interpretations. They are not official provider mascots.

These characters ship in the Mac Models screen from 0.3.0 alpha 3 onward. The old 12 × 12 drawings remain on this page for comparison. Edition details identify a model version; Clepso’s usage-based levels remain separate.

The drawings use an integer pixel grid, a consistent foot anchor and a restrained palette. Only the selected portrait moves. Pause and Reduce Motion controls are available; model data is not needed to explore this page. From 0.4.0 alpha 1, a native flourish marks newly crossed output levels while viewing a model. The website’s button remains a demonstration and does not change usage.
